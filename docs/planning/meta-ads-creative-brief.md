# Domani Meta Ads Creative Brief

**Status:** In production

**Owners:** Phil and Sami

**Campaign:** Stage 1 US iOS Meta test

**Target launch:** August 11, 2026

## Creative Goal

Show a busy adult how one short evening-planning habit can make the next morning feel clearer. The app and the outcome should be understandable without sound.

The first test will use three different ideas, not three crops of the same video.

## Available Material

- One third-party-produced video
- 9:16, 4:5, and 1:1 versions of that same video
- Screenshots currently used in the App Store listing

The three video layouts should be assigned to their matching placements within one video ad.

## Stage 1 Concepts

### A. Morning Clarity Video

**Job:** Introduce the core before-and-after story.

**Message:** Plan tonight. Wake up knowing what matters.

**Asset:** Existing video in three placement layouts.

Before approval, confirm:

- the first three seconds make the problem or outcome clear;
- Domani or the app interface appears early;
- captions make the story understandable without sound;
- text stays inside placement-safe areas;
- the trial and price wording matches the live app; and
- the final call to action sends the viewer to the App Store.

### B. Evening Habit Static

**Job:** Make Domani feel simpler than a large productivity system.

**Message:** Take a few minutes tonight to decide what matters tomorrow.

**Asset:** One current planning-screen screenshot presented in a clean 4:5 feed layout and 9:16 Story/Reels layout.

Suggested overlay:

> Plan tomorrow tonight.

Suggested supporting line:

> Wake up with your priorities already decided.

### C. Product Proof and Offer

**Job:** Show that Domani is a real, focused app and explain the offer after the product value is clear.

**Message:** A focused daily plan, a 14-day trial, and no recurring subscription.

**Asset:** A static ad or short carousel using current App Store screenshots. If a carousel is unavailable in the selected App Promotion setup, use one strong screenshot-led static instead.

Suggested sequence:

1. Plan tomorrow tonight.
2. Choose what matters most.
3. Wake up ready.
4. Try it free for 14 days.
5. Early-adopter lifetime price: $9.99. No subscription.

## Copy Rules

- Lead with the user's morning or evening experience, not a feature list.
- Keep the primary text short enough to understand at a glance.
- Use plain calls to action such as `Try Domani free` or `Start your 14-day trial`.
- Say `no payment method required upfront` only when space allows and the statement remains accurate.
- Do not call $34.99 the regular, former, or discounted-from price until it has become the established selling price.
- Do not use `limited time only` until the calendar end date is approved and operationally enforceable.
- Do not use fabricated testimonials, exaggerated urgency, or guaranteed outcomes.

## App Store Alignment Checklist

Phil and Sami should review the live US App Store page on the same phone a prospect would use and answer each item before launch.

### Message Match

- [ ] The app name and subtitle describe a daily or evening-planning product.
- [ ] The first screenshot supports `Plan tomorrow tonight` or the morning-clarity outcome.
- [ ] The first three screenshots show the planning flow, priority selection, and next-day benefit.
- [ ] The screenshot order tells a simple story without requiring the full description.
- [ ] The screenshots used in ads also appear in, or clearly match, the store experience.

### Product Accuracy

- [ ] Every visible screen matches the current app.
- [ ] Feature names match the words used inside the app.
- [ ] The listing does not promise a feature that is unavailable in the live build.
- [ ] The first app experience delivers the behavior shown in the ad.

### Offer Accuracy

- [ ] The live in-app product is $9.99.
- [ ] The 14-day trial is described accurately.
- [ ] The listing clearly states that no payment method is required upfront.
- [ ] The listing clearly states that the purchase is lifetime access, not a subscription.
- [ ] The listing does not show $34.99 as a previous price.
- [ ] The ad, store listing, onboarding, and purchase screen use compatible wording.

### Conversion Readiness

- [ ] The App Store page loads from every ad preview.
- [ ] The strongest screenshot is first.
- [ ] Screenshots remain readable on a phone-sized screen.
- [ ] Ratings and review count are recorded before launch.
- [ ] Sign-in, trial creation, and purchase display have been tested on the live build.

## Approval

Both owners should review final ad previews in Meta, not only the source files. An asset is ready when the text, crop, safe area, destination, offer, and app experience are correct in the actual preview.
