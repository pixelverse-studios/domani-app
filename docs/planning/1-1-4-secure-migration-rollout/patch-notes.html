More reliable account access

October 20, 2026Partially complete

Overview

Account access improvements are prepared for the next Domani release. They are not available in the public app yet.

Added

  • A clear update prompt can guide people to the store when a newer release becomes required.

Improved

  • Purchase recovery handles delayed confirmations and refunds more consistently.
  • Account changes keep earlier sign-in actions tied to the account that started them.

Fixed

  • Repeated purchase notifications no longer leave access updates stuck after an interruption.

What happens next

  • These improvements will become available after the release rollout is complete.
